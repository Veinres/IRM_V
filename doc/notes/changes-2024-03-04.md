# Changes with generalised power balance equation

Joel Fischer, 2024-04-04

When changing the power balance equation to be generalised (the same for all materials), some changes could not be avoided. This is an attempt to list all of them. 

 
concerning commit f26c0b886a15b249da5c5ef9060f1684bba1fec0
from 2024-03-04
"Generalize power balance equation for all materials."

--

- In the previous version, the hot and cold ionization reactions from to Arii were missing from the coll\_loss term (izcAri, izhAri. izhGSAri). These are autmatically included in the new version and account for the discrepancies in the ArTi material system.

- For all target/working gas combinations, only the last Penning reaction was considered in the pervious version (mistakenly, a reaction tag was used instead of a range of reactions). This is fixed in the new version.

- The hot and cold ionization reactions from Cum{1,2,3} to Cui are missing from the izh\_cost and hot2cold are missing in both the previous and the new version. Should these be included? This might again be a question regarding whether these things should be taken into account in the effective cost calculation or explicitly. I believe, in this case this might actually be a mistake. The Cum{1,2,3} species do have an entry in the EC, but it is not used. The relevant reactions are (iz{c,h}Cum{1,2,3}). These are not yet included in the new version.

- Usually, the energy gained through deexcitation is accounted for explicitly in deex{c,h}. However, for copper in the previous version, this was not the case. They also don't appear to be acocunted for in the EC. The relevant reactions are (ddex{c,h}Cum{1,2,3}). These are included in the new version.
    - However, the energy associated with the deexcitation reactions from a higher metastable state to a lower metastable state do not have the correct energy gain, since the energy of the products is not taken into account. This concerns the extra dex{c,h}Cum3 reactions and is not yet fixed in the new version.

- Arguably, since we're including deexcitation from Cum3 to Cum{1,2}, it might make sense to do the same for all pairwise excitation/deexciation reacitons if the CS can be obtained. This is definitely not a priority right now.

- Arguably, the Penning reactions involving metastable Ar and metastable Cu could/should also be added if the CS can be obtained. These might even produce Ari. This is definitely not a priority right now.

- The double ionization reaction from GS Ar to Ari should produces two electrons, however this is not considered in neither the previous nor the new version. This should probably be done in the reactions table / Rea struct.
